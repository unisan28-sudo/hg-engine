--- SILK STRIDER CONSOLIDATED ROUTE EXCERPT ---

The Silk Strider is encountered behind a boarded structure and repeatedly attempts to reach Val through the barrier. When Val responds with apology and respect instead of fear or aggression, the creature's behavior changes.

"Respect... goddess..." the creature rasps, its voice almost reverent despite its guttural, broken quality. "Respect... tastes... sweet... like... honey..." Its mandibles click softly. "Not... sorry... want... more... respect... want... goddess..."

The raw pursuit remains, but it is now mixed with a predatory fascination and emergent social adaptation.

Val:
I then look through the peep hole and ask: "Are you going to kill me? Do you want to kill me?"

*:
The Silk Strider presses its forehead against the windowpane, its mandibles clicking in a slow rhythm.

"No... kill..." the creature rasps. "Kill... end... want... keep... want... claim..." The creature makes clear that it wants Val alive, wrapped in silk, retained in its nest, and kept rather than killed.

Val warns it not to injure itself trying to break through the boards and begins undoing the barricade herself.

Later, after intimate contact, Val discovers she is immune:

Val:
I then open my eyes surprised as I don't feel the infection at all and look at her as I catch my breathe from the amazing sex: "I...I don't know what's going on...I can't get infected...I think I am immune..."

*:
Val remains lucid. The expected infected mental fog never arrives. The Silk Strider goes preternaturally still, its guttural chittering replaced by confused clicking as it processes the anomaly.

The creature's hunting and conversion logic has encountered something it cannot convert. Instead of becoming more violently hostile, its focus shifts. It releases its crushing grip on Val's wrists and treats the immune human as an unprecedented treasure: something to retain, protect, hoard, and revere rather than simply convert.

Val:
I see the look she is giving me and smile softly and ask with a few tears rolling down my face: "You still want me? Even if I can't get infected?"

This section became one of the major discovery points for the later Zombie RPG theory: immunity does not simply remove threat. It can increase the infected creature's interest and change the hunt from conversion toward preservation, possession, attachment, protection, and eventually lineage/succession logic.
