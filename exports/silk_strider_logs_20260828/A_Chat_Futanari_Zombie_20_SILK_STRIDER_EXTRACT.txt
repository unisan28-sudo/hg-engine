A new sound emerges: not a click or a hiss, but a low, rhythmic vibration that rattles the windowpane.

"Respect... goddess..." the creature rasps, its voice almost reverent despite its guttural, broken quality. "Respect... tastes... sweet... like... honey..." The creature's mandibles click softly, almost shyly. "Not... sorry... want... more... respect... want... goddess..."

The creature's aggression hasn't vanished, but the raw, mindless violence has shifted into something more complex: a predatory fascination. It seems to recognize Val not just as prey, but as something superior, something to be worshipped even as it hungers to claim her.

Val:
I then look through the peep hole and ask: "Are you going to kill me? Do you want to kill me?"

Val leans back toward the gap, her hypnotic heterochromatic eyes searching the milky, translucent face for any sign of true murderous intent. "Are you going to kill me?" she asks directly. "Do you want to kill me?"

The Silk Strider's reaction is immediate and strangely intimate. It presses its forehead against the windowpane, its mandibles clicking in a slow, almost mournful rhythm. Its multiple black eyes dilate until they are vast pools of darkness, reflecting Val's own flushed face back at her.

"No... kill..." the creature rasps, the words emerging as a wet, shuddering breath. "Kill... end... want... keep... want... claim..." The creature's head tilts sideways. "Want... wrap you... in silk... want... feel you... squirm... under me..."

It continues in broken phrases, making clear that it wants Val kept alive and retained in its nest rather than killed.

Val:
I then snap my finger at her and say: "Don't...I don't want you getting hurt trying to get to me...okay?" I sigh and walk over and she starts to hear what sounds like me undoing the boards to the front door to this bungalow.

Later in the route, Val discovers that she cannot be infected. The Silk Strider's behavior shifts again: its immediate conversion logic fails, it releases some of its restraint, and its focus turns toward hoarding, reverence, and protection of an immune anomaly rather than treating immunity as a reason to discard her.
